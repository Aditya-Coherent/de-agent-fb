# Review findings, 2026-10-15

This is a review of the 5 CMI whitepapers in `AiAgentsFleet/output/<slug>/`. It
was done by reading each report against its `cmi_model.json`, its
`whitepaper_fields.json` (`fields_raw` = agent output before jitter, `fields` =
after) and domain knowledge.

Claims marked **(check)** come from model knowledge and need confirming against
a source. Treat everything else as a confirmed defect.

Use this file as the **regression list**. After the plans are implemented and
the reports regenerated (PLAN-10), each item must be gone or explicitly justified.

## B. Agricultural Biostimulants

Headline: $4.60B (2025) sits between Precedence ($4.87B) and Verified ($3.95B), so it is OK once the jitter is gone.

1. "African Union's Fertilizer and Soil Health Action Plan 2023-2033" is wrong; it is the **2024–2034** plan (Nairobi, May 2024). (PLAN-06)
2. "local distribution and technical support shape share more than global brand scale" contradicts the data: regional/other is 35.7% in MEA vs 47.4% globally. (PLAN-07)
3. Vendor shares look wrong. (PLAN-05)
   - Isagro 4.4% global (~$200M) and BASF 8.0% (~$370M) are not credible.
   - Italpollina is now Hello Nature, and Biolchim is owned by Huber; neither is noted.
   - "Recent acquisition of Arysta" refers to a 2018-19 deal.
4. Microbial is the 2nd-smallest global type ($485M, 9.4%) but the largest in most ME/Africa countries. "Cereals & Grains … largest … and fastest" goes against published views (F&V usually largest). Turf & Ornamentals at 11% looks high. (PLAN-04)
5. "Rest of" plug at 12.0% (X6): Rest of Europe $236M < Spain. Rest of LatAm $62.4M < Argentina. (PLAN-03)
6. Unsupported claims are reused as findings: "Middle East … is where precision foliar use is expanding fastest". Action 1 (Egypt and Morocco trials) cites total Africa size, while Morocco is $6.27M. The trend "Government Food Security" talks only about export rules. (PLAN-07)
7. "tightened fertilizer registration protocols … sustain demand" is illogical, since tighter rules are a barrier. (PLAN-07)
8. Regulators (check), all PLAN-06:
   - Turkey: BÜGEM, not TAGEM.
   - Israel: Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security.
   - Nigeria: National Fertilizer Quality Control Act 2019.
   - Egypt "Pesticides Committee" registering biostimulants is doubtful.
   - Kenya: PCPB.
9. Country sizes (check): Germany > France/Italy/Spain; Latin America slowest region; Israel $5.55M and Morocco $6.27M vs Turkey $42.9M. (PLAN-03)
10. Saudi and UAE segment subsections are one-sentence fragments. "About CMI" omits agriculture. Grammar: "Microbial Biostimulants remains". (PLAN-07)
